## Projects

### Ringin
**Solo project** · Shipped, then sunset · *Private; demos on my portfolio*

A B2B sales dialer for outbound teams. Agents call from the browser over Twilio WebRTC, and each call goes out from the healthiest caller ID, scored on pickup rate, call length and dial spacing to keep numbers off carrier spam lists. Multi-tenant API with 200+ endpoints.

`Next.js` `Hono` `PostgreSQL` `Drizzle` `Twilio` `Clerk`

### [Shortlist](https://github.com/abdelelgendy/Internship-app-tracker)
**Open source** · My own job-search copilot

Finds postings from company job boards, scores how well each one fits, prepares applications for me to review and submit, tracks follow-ups, and runs interview practice. Local-first: my data never leaves my machine.

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Node.js` `Playwright`

### Mentor Q&A Bot
**Applied machine learning** · *Private; demos on my portfolio*

A Discord bot that answers a community's questions using its mentor's past answers and course videos. It turns each question into a sentence embedding, finds the closest past answers with semantic search, and declines when nothing is relevant enough instead of guessing. I tuned that cutoff with F1 scoring and bootstrap resampling, and transcribed the course videos with Whisper so they could be searched too.

`Python` `sentence-transformers` `ChromaDB` `Whisper` `Claude API` `discord.py`

### Trading Co-Pilot
**Python** · *Private; demos on my portfolio*

An assistant for a futures trader. It watches the market every 15 minutes, flags trade setups, sizes each position from current volatility, enforces daily loss limits with a circuit breaker, and sends alerts to a dashboard and Telegram. It never places orders: the trader decides. Covered by 577 automated tests.

`Python` `FastAPI` `SQLite` `Next.js` `pytest`
